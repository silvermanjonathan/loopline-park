A brand-new theme park, five afternoons, one junior engineering crew. Every day you build a working piece of the park in Python — and on Friday, your own attraction opens to the public.

How the week works

Each day starts with a Park Briefing, runs two build blocks with a break at the Fast-Pass Lounge, and ends with a Test Ride (show your work) and a Maintenance Log (exit ticket). Tap a day below to open its engineering manual.
